SAFETY & COMPLIANCE

Safety isn't a line item. It's the first stage.

Every project starts with a Job Hazard Analysis and closes with a documented safety record — no exceptions, regardless of scope size.

JHA COMPLIANCE

A hazard analysis before a single cable is pulled.

Every site — from a single retail closet to a full data hall build — gets a Job Hazard Analysis completed before mobilization. Hazards, controls, and site-specific protocols are documented and briefed to the crew before work begins, and revisited whenever site conditions change.

WSIB COVERAGE

Full coverage, verified on every job.

All Netech field personnel are covered under current WSIB clearance. Certificates are verified before each project and available to your site on request — one less compliance check for your team to chase.

PPE STANDARDS

Enforced, not optional.

Hard hats, safety footwear, high-visibility clothing, and site-specific protective equipment are mandatory for every technician, every site, every day — checked by crew leads before work starts.
