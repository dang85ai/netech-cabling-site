WHO WE ARE

The silent partner behind your infrastructure delivery.

Netech was built on a simple premise: the general contractor's name is the one on the line, so the subcontractor's job is to make sure it never gets questioned. We operate as an extension of your team — our vans can run unbranded, our field staff can wear your colours, and our paperwork can carry your letterhead.

Over ten years we've grown from a regional cabling crew into a nationwide telecommunications infrastructure operation: 65+ technicians, more than 200 completed projects, and coverage that reaches every province our GC partners build in. What hasn't changed is the model — one accountable team, one point of contact, and a delivery standard that holds regardless of city or site count.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

One neck to choke.

Accountability doesn't work if it's shared across a committee. So we built the org chart around a single line of responsibility for every project.

Single PM ownership One project manager owns scope, schedule, and communication from kickoff to closeout — no rotating point of contact.
Deliverables department A dedicated team compiles test results, as-builts, and closeout documentation so nothing is chased down after the fact.
Coordination team Scheduling and trade coordination run through a central desk, keeping our crews aligned with every other trade on site.
